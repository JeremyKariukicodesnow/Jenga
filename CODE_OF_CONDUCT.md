# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique code and engineering evidence, not people. Welcome newcomers and respect differing backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, spam and sharing private information without consent are not accepted. Keep discussions relevant; do not misrepresent affiliation or engineering certification.

Report conduct concerns privately to Jeremy through the maintainer's GitHub profile; request a private channel before sharing sensitive details. For vulnerabilities, use SECURITY.md. The maintainer may remove content, warn participants, limit access or ban accounts according to severity and context. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and protect reporter confidentiality where possible.

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests and project-managed spaces. It does not promise confidentiality beyond what the platform and applicable law permit.
